Terms and privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What Status: GO is

Status: GO is a free educational mission simulator made by team One Pixel Home (Lima, Peru) for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It is a student prototype, not a professional engineering tool. Some values are simplified or illustrative, and every number in the simulator is tagged with where it comes from.

Not affiliated with NASA

Status: GO is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. NASA does not endorse any non-U.S. Government entity. NASA data and images are used under NASA's media and data guidelines and are credited where they appear (Earth image: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation).

Using the simulator

  • You can use Status: GO for free, for learning and teaching. No account is needed.
  • It is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. We may change it, or stop running it, at any time.
  • Please don't try to break it, overload it or misuse the leaderboard.
  • The source code is released under the MIT License.

The leaderboard

Saving a score is optional. If you save one, we store:

  • the name you type (2–24 characters; please don't use your full real name or anyone else's),
  • your mission design (mission, orbit, instrument, power, downlink and rocket), the team's votes and team size,
  • the score, which our server calculates, and the date and time.

The name, score, mission, orbit, team size and date are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Names that break our name rules are rejected, and we may remove any entry. The data is stored in a database on Microsoft Azure. Your IP address is used only for a few minutes, in memory, to limit how often scores can be saved; we do not store it.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no cookies and no analytics in the simulator.
  • The site is hosted on Azure Static Web Apps; like any web host, Microsoft may keep standard technical logs.
  • The demo page embeds our video from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). If you play it, YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Questions or removal requests

To ask a question, or to have a leaderboard entry removed, open an issue at github.com/OnePixelHome/NASA/issues and tell us the name and date of the entry.
