✓ Do

  • Pair every color with an icon and a text label.
  • Use Fredoka with tabular figures for every number.
  • Keep hit areas at 44 px, even on small chips.
  • Illustrations: flat vector, rounded shapes, thick soft outlines, token palette only.
  • Use hand-made SVG for interface elements.
  • Mark any AI-generated background with a visible “AI-generated” label.

✕ Don’t

  • No NASA logos, insignias, flags or mission patches.
  • Don’t put muted text on accent fills; use page ink (#0B1026).
  • Don’t remove the focus ring or rely on hover alone.
  • Don’t use AI imagery for UI, icons or characters.
  • Don’t animate with overshoot when reduced motion is on.
