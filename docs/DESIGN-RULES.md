# Space Apps 2026 — Design & art rules

For Thais · One Pixel Home · Status: GO
Source: official NASA Space Apps *Project Submission Guide* and *Judging & Awards Guide* (2026). Quotes are verbatim.

| Topic | Rule | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Demo format | Video **≤ 30 s** or slides **≤ 7** (incl. title + intro). "Projects that exceed these limits will not be eligible for Global Judging." | Belle makes both; your visuals must fit those limits |
| Language | Everything in **English**. Videos need **English subtitles**; slides must be in English | All text in visuals, slides and the game in English |
| Slide content | Must show: **team name, members' names, the challenge selected**; an image of the project, the user experience, how NASA data makes it possible | Plan visuals for those slides |
| Slide style | "Readable font size", "limit the number of words on each slide", "strong visuals" | Big type, few words, screenshots of the game |
| SI images & video | SI-generated images and videos "**must include a visible watermark**" | Every Claude Design image, Gemini Flow clip or HeyGen frame → visible **"AI-generated"** label |
| NASA branding | SI content "may not use, contain, or modify any NASA branding or design elements (such as the NASA logos, flags, or mission identifiers)" | No NASA logo, meatball, worm, flags or mission patches. Avoid them entirely, not just in SI work |
| Minors | No "images, video, audio, or other likeness to persons under 18" | No kids in any image, even though our users are students |
| Copyright | "Not used any copyrighted materials (music, images, text, etc.) that we don't have permission to use." Every external resource, **even free ones**, goes in References | Only free/licensed fonts, icons and images. Send Belle the name + licence of each one |
| Original work | We tick "Confirm All Work is Original" | Design comes from us; SI is a tool, and SI "should not be used to come up with your idea" |
| Project page images | JPG, PNG or GIF, **max 10 MB** each. Videos and documents can't be uploaded | Export page screenshots/graphics in those formats |
| Hosting | Video and slides via a **public link**, "no password, permission, or registration" | Files shared publicly, tested in incognito |
| Judging: Presentation | Scored 1–5: "well-structured and compelling", "engaging storytelling"; judges evaluate **the demo and the full project page** | Visuals count on the page too, not only in the video |
| Awards for design | **Art & Technology Award**: "most effectively combines technical and creative skills". **Best Storytelling Award** | Our design can aim at these two |
| Deadline | Submit before **Sun Nov 15, 23:59 Lima**. Team target: **Sun 20:00**, code freeze 18:00 | Final visuals before Sun 17:00 |
